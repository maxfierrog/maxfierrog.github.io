i enter and hear hopeful tones 
from aspects of you composed; 
with restraint chosen out of many, 
allowed to exist by your hands

in another room of your house 
your words become tulip petals; 
lightly into the air you pronounce 
your voice in a violet spectrum

outside, the clouds always wander; 
in an ephemeral december window, 
your image trembles through water 
of raindrops chosen by your words

i don't spill these thoughts, i hold 
a sunday evening in crystal fixed 
refracting your luminous warmth: 
an original gift of your goodness

rivers of ice, pillars of glass, 
like water, light runs through crystal; 
thoughts travel inside your forehead: 
in my mind, to think is now to reason 
the precious movements of your will:
across the earth
    glacial penmanship, 
    beautiful writing

springs of magma, columns of light, 
deep in those channels, molten flows; 
warm blood filters through your dermis: 
on my chest, to feel is now to witness 
waves of sun washing over your skin: 
across the earth
    volcanic flows, 
    solar embrace

turbulent flames over the water,
soothing stillness hugs me fixed-
come evening, you dress me
in ever more radiant skin,
        now warm evermore:
of all the earthly pillars built from rock,
infinite is the throne in which we now lay;
    high
waterfall of light, one endless flow,
it washes through insufficient hands:
  always insufficient 
    yet now complete 
    and forever more:
we phosphoresce in our presence,
               glowing evermore:
everything shines over the water-
set ablaze and held calmly still:

    Everywhere,
your presence is replicated
in iridescent bubbles, floating
    full of reflections:

    I find you in explorations
    of the frontier, lost incursions 
           of language into concepts.

          Those thoughts 
    form a massive tree:
        from its sprouting branches bloom
        flowers of hands whose fingers unfold,
      touching you for the first time once again:
      exploring your surface
          scattered across the world of ideas,
          in textures of endless definition:
    with ten thousand hands I hold you,
    I spill my consciousness thin
        over your infinite face,
        iridescing in the sun:

    when you look at me
    I dissolve in the intensity 
              of a single word: 

      my world is in your eyes,
      so full of reflections.

        Always,
     time flows towards you until it reaches you:
        a pool of water at your feet
        will soon be an ocean of time.

    When I am away from you,
    the fruit of the tree slowly
        loses its vibrant signal,
        carried in tears of time
           running down my face:

        so I fight for you,
        my senses heighten to extremes
        forging thought into your form,
    to arrive at you in my mind,
    to be with you for an instant
         that pops like a bubble:
        leaving behind images of you 
        that reflect over the water:
          they echo in my mind:
            resonating signals,
            compounding beauty.
    
  Time will flow until I find you 
  in the next instant, once again:
    together eternally,
    floating tenacious.

    Everywhere and always,
I am completely in love with you,
                        Julia.
