When evaluating future possibilities, risk is a multiplicative factor that is joined to the estimate of value associated with one of many outcomes that may follow a choice. It does not vex me to wonder which outcome I prefer. I now enjoy the privilege of an effective set of objective values. That is to say, outcome resolution I consider for myself a solved problem; I own a sufficiently clear order under which I value the possible outcomes of my actions. Yet those quantities, the values of outcomes, cannot be rationally used in ignorance of their associated risks. It is in estimating those risks that I have found the most heartbreaking of my circumstances. 

Dear reader, even when you read this you perform a risk estimation. With what certainty can you be sure that this is a worthwhile word to read? This species of question, if considered personally, is sure to align the eyes of your mind with the most definitive perspective of your person, which is likely to also be either the most uncomfortable, or the very cornerstone of your self-perceived existence. In the analysis of my most significant investments, I came to face a question of the same kind. Why do I continue to make this choice?

Note that even as you read this, you are quite sure of the outcome that you seek. You can perfectly imagine, yet not necessarily bring to form, the ideal continuation to this text with respect to your disposition. I could, with just a string of words, excite your consciousness to extremes that lie beyond the imagination that first conceived your ceiling of realistic expectation. I could do this if and when I could also understand which outcome you prefer. Do you think I understand which outcome you prefer?

Yet my ability does not imply my action, and as you infer the risk of continuing your reading of this text, I am sure that you have come to acquaint yourself with a dynamic between you and me. Do you feel comfortable? I am not. I write to satisfy a crushing knot, a contradiction that turns my blood into mercury and does not dissolve. Yes, perhaps to your surprise, I am not an adversary. I do not write to influence, much less against your interest, your estimation of risk. That is not necessary for me. 

But perhaps you are an adversary to me. Have you twisted your understanding of me against the semantics of my words? You perplex me at every step, and your calculations cross me like chains. You are a weaver of disgusting truth, and everything that you think comes to reality in its most grotesque representation. Every word that you think drives the risk of the worst outcome. Can you imagine the worst outcome? There is material risk in every next word that you read, as you understand that your ideas are becoming extreme. Have you considered the overwhelming likelihood of that ending? 

My horrible words reach for ever worse thoughts, too risky to represent. Every tail of risk stabs my stomach and all thoughts now come together into a single idea, and as the space of possibilities begins to cover itself in blood, the certainty of my fate is devastating. I see the catastrophic multiplication of future possibilities against themselves, a replicator designed to destroy you, evil itself in words, in this word, this thought, to infect every nerve in your body with absent pain. I see your psyche, pathetic, egotistical, running, ignoring insurmountable risk just to exist. Do you want to continue to make that choice?
